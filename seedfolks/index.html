2Meet the storyteller

Seedfolks is a short novel from 1997. Thirteen neighbors in Cleveland, Ohio each tell one chapter. They never planned it, but together they turn a vacant lot full of junk into a garden.

Chapter 1 belongs to Kim. She is nine years old. Her family came from Vietnam. Her father died in Vietnam before she was born, so she has never met him. In her home there is an altar with his photograph, and every year on the anniversary of his death the family burns incense and remembers him.

Her father was a farmer. Kim is not. That fact is the whole engine of this chapter.

Watch for this: Kim never says the words "I miss my father." She shows it instead, with six beans and a spoon. Good readers notice what a character does, not only what a character says.

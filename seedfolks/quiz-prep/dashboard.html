🌱 Seedfolks — Quiz Practice 1 (group, Claude leads)

One slide per question from the paper version: Part 1 spelling (good, woman, old, bury, dig + two sentences), Part 2 four picture sentences, Part 3 capitals K/I/M, spelled words (Seedfolks, Garden, Empty lot), “Ana is an old woman.”, circle the period / capital, then spell-out-loud. Students write on paper; after Check they tap 👍 when their paper matches. “Not yet” taps are the misses. Score = slides marked 👍 out of 21 (spell-out-loud counts once 3 words are spelled).

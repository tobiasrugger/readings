🦁 Group Practice — make a deck

Fill in only what you need — every box you fill becomes slides. Claude (the lion) leads the group: it reads each item aloud, the group writes on paper, then Check shows the answer and the group taps 👍. Directions appear in English plus Chinese, Spanish, Thai, Vietnamese, Nepali and Arabic.

For a story or news article: paste the words, sentences and questions from it below. Or ask Claude in the Launch Plus chat to write the deck as a file — it can draw pictures for every word.

1 · Name it

2 · Spelling (I say it, you write it)

3 · Letters

4 · Pictures to describe

Upload a photo or drawing. Add two or three good sentences — they show after Check (students write their own first).

5 · Talk, then choose (multiple choice)

One per line: question | right answer | wrong | wrong | wrong | hint | why. The answers are mixed up for each student. A wrong answer fades and shows your hint — the answer is never given away.

6 · Questions to answer in writing

One per line: question | good answer | another good answer

7 · Spell out loud (take turns)

8 · Translations

The standard directions are already translated. Your own words (opening words, questions, hints, “why” lines) get translated here — fix any you want, then save. Anything left empty is translated on the student’s screen.

9 · Save

Mix several decks into one practice

Tick decks in the order you want them played. Each keeps its own slides; there is one Turn in at the end.

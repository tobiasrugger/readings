From A to Z

You know the alphabet. Now spell out loud, like a strong reader and writer. About 30 minutes.

Six parts: Read, Fix the sentence, Bigger words, Spell it, Write, Record.

Tap an answer. If you are wrong, you can try again, but the first try is your score.

Keep this page in English. Use Tap words to see one word in your language.

1 · Read

Linh has to spell her name at the office. Tap Listen. The words light up as you hear them. The yellow words are from your book. Tap a yellow word to see it in your language.

Say it with Amira

Listen to the sentence. Press the red microphone and say the same sentence. Then hear Amira and hear yourself. Try them all.

Read it as a paragraph

Now answer the questions.

2 · Fix the sentence

Each whiteboard has one mistake. Tap the wrong word. Then tap the correct form.

3 · Bigger words

Strong readers know more than one word for the same idea. Every right answer climbs one rung of the ladder.

4 · Spell it

Listen to the letters. Which name is it? Tap the name you hear.

5 · Write

Three steps. Each one is a little bigger. Spell carefully: capital letters where they belong. Your teacher reads this part.

6 · Record

Tap the speaker to listen. Tap the red button, spell the word out loud, tap again. The page writes what it hears.

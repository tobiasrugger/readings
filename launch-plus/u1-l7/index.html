Class Acts

What do you do at school? Learn eight action words, use he plays and I play the right way, and don’t be afraid to ask for help. About 30 minutes.

Six parts: Read, Fix the sentence, Bigger words, He or I?, Write, Record.

Tap an answer. If you are wrong, read the hint and try again.

Keep this page in English. Use Tap words to see one word in your language.

1 · Read

Mateo and Amira talk about school. Tap Listen. The words light up as you hear them. The yellow words are from your book. Tap a yellow word to see it in your language.

Say it with Amira

Listen to the sentence. Press the red microphone and say the same sentence. Then hear Amira and hear yourself. Try them all.

The whole story

This is the same story about Mateo and Amira. Now it is one paragraph. Read it again.

Now answer the questions.

2 · Fix the sentence

Each whiteboard has one mistake. Tap the wrong word. Then tap the correct form.

3 · Bigger words

Strong readers know more than one word for the same idea. Every right answer climbs one rung of the ladder.

4 · He or I?

He plays. I play. Look at the picture: who does it? One person (he, she, a name) adds -s. I, we and they do not. Tap the right verb.

5 · Write

Three steps. Each one is a little bigger. After I, use the base verb. After he or she, add -s. Your teacher reads this part.

6 · Record

Tap the speaker to listen. Tap the red button, say it, tap again. The page writes what it hears.

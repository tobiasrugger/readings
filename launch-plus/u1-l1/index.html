People at School

You know the words. Now use them like a strong reader and writer. About 25 minutes.

Five parts: Read, Fix the sentence, Bigger words, Zoom in, Write.

1 · Read

2 · Fix the sentence

3 · Bigger words

Strong readers know more than one word for the same idea. Match the book word to a bigger word, or build a new word from it.

4 · Zoom in

A school has many people. Read what each person does. Tap the job.

5 · Write

Three steps. Each one is a little bigger. Your teacher reads this part.

From A to Z · Extras

More practice with letters and names. About 20 minutes.

Five parts: Capitals, Next letter, Big or small, ABC order, Record.

Tap an answer. If you are wrong, you can try again, but the first try is your score.

1 · Capitals

Twelve words need a capital letter: the first word of a sentence, names of people, places, and languages. Tap each word to fix it.

2 · Next letter

Which letter comes next? Say the alphabet in your head. Tap the letter.

3 · Big or small

Uppercase = big letter (A). Lowercase = small letter (a). Vowels are a, e, i, o, u. Tap the answer.

4 · ABC order

Look at the first letter of each name. Which name comes first in alphabetical order? Tap it.

5 · Record

Tap the speaker to listen. Tap the red button, say the letters or the name, tap again. The page writes what it hears.

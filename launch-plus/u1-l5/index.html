About Me

You can say your name. Now tell someone who you are, like a strong reader and writer. About 30 minutes.

Six parts: Read, Fix the sentence, Bigger words, Pronouns, Write, Record.

Tap an answer. If you are wrong, you can try again, but the first try is your score.

Keep this page in English. Use Tap words to see one word in your language.

1 · Read

Mateo interviews Amira. Tap Listen. The words light up as you hear them. The yellow words are from your book. Tap a yellow word to see it in your language.

Say it with Amira

Listen to the sentence. Press the red microphone and say the same sentence. Then hear Amira and hear yourself. Try all five.

The whole story

This is the same story about Mateo and Amira. Now it is one paragraph. Read it again.

Now answer the questions.

2 · Fix the sentence

Each whiteboard has one mistake. Tap the wrong word. Then tap the correct form.

3 · Bigger words

Strong readers know more than one word for the same idea. Every right answer climbs one rung of the ladder.

4 · Which pronoun?

A pronoun is a short word that takes the place of a name. Read the sentence. Look at the picture. Tap the right pronoun.

5 · Write

Three steps. Each one is a little bigger. Step 3 is your own four lines. Your teacher reads this part.

6 · Record

Tap the speaker to listen. Tap the red button, say the sentence, tap again. The page writes what it hears.

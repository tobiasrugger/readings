School Supplies

You know pen and pencil. Now say how many you need, ask to borrow one, and spell the plural right. About 30 minutes.

Six parts: Read, Fix the sentence, Bigger words, One or more?, Write, Record.

Tap an answer. If you are wrong, read the hint and try again.

Keep this page in English. Use Tap words to see one word in your language.

1 · Read

Jun needs school supplies. Tap Listen. The words light up as you hear them. The yellow words are from your book. Tap a yellow word to see it in your language.

Say it with Linh

Listen to the sentence. Press the red microphone and say the same sentence. Then hear Linh and hear yourself. Try them all.

The whole story

This is the same story about Jun and Linh. Now it is one paragraph. Read it again.

Now answer the questions.

2 · Fix the sentence

Each whiteboard has one mistake. Tap the wrong word. Then tap the correct form.

3 · Bigger words

Strong readers know more than one word for the same idea. Every right answer climbs one rung of the ladder.

4 · One or more?

One pencil, two pencils. Listen. Look at the pictures: one on the left, two on the right. Tap the word for two.

5 · Write

Three steps. Each one is a little bigger. Add -s or -es when there is more than one. Your teacher reads this part.

6 · Record

Tap the speaker to listen. Tap the red button, say it, tap again. The page writes what it hears.

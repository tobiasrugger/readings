3. DIRECTIONS: Look at the pictures from the movie. Write a sentence and a question about each picture. Use capital letters, periods and PAST tense.

Write 2 questions using “didn’t.”   Why didn’t…?   Who didn’t…?   When didn’t…?

Example: Why didn’t the boys tell their parents about the trip?

4. DIRECTIONS: Answer the questions using “didn’t” or the past tense.

EXAMPLE: Did Gordie live in a big city?  →  No, Gordie didn’t live in a big city.

5. DIRECTIONS: Translate each sentence. Then turn each sentence into a question.

IMPORTANT: When we write questions, we often need “did,” “does” or “do.”
Present: Why do/does…   When do/does…   Where do/does…   How do/does…
Past: Why did…   When did…   Where did…   How did…   Why didn’t…   Who didn’t…

Example: The boys walked twenty miles.  →  How far did the boys walk?

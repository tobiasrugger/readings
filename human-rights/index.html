Day 1

I see. I think. I wonder.

Look carefully. Say what is really there before you say what it means.

How this works. Look at each picture for one minute. Write three things. I see = only what your eyes see. I think = what you believe is happening. I wonder = a question you have.

Careful: "I see a sad boy" is really I think. Your eyes see a boy. Sad is what you think.

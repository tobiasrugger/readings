Music Words 1: How does it sound?

Words for speed, volume, high and low, the parts of a song, and the instruments you hear. You will use these words to talk about your favorite song.

    Now you write

    Think of any song you know. Describe the sound.

    • The song is called ______________.
    • The tempo is (fast / slow).
    • The music is (loud / quiet).
    • The singer's voice is (high / low).
    • I can hear a ______________ (instrument).
    • The song ______________ has a ______________ tempo.
    • In this song I can hear ______________ and ______________.
    • The singer's voice is ______________.
    • My favorite part is the (chorus / verse) because ______________.
    • Describe the sound of the song in 4–5 sentences. Use at least five words from this lesson.
    • Compare the verse and the chorus. How is the sound different?
    • Which instrument is the most important in this song? Why do you think so?

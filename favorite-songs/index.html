Our Favorite Songs

Everybody brings one song. We learn the words to talk about music, then we build a class mixtape and listen to each other.

Sign in once, up at the top. Put in your school email and your name. Every page in this unit remembers you, so you can start on a Chromebook and finish on your phone.
How this unit works. Do the two word lessons first — they give you the English you need. Then add your song. When everybody is finished, the class mixtape fills up and you can listen and comment on each other's songs.

What you have to write about your song

  1. Why do you like this song?
  2. When did you first hear it?
  3. What is the main idea? Why did the singer write it?
  4. What is your favorite line? Why?

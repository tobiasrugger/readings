Why do you like this song?

  • I like this song because it is ______________.
  • It makes me feel ______________.
  • I like the ______________ (beat / melody / lyrics / voice).
  • I like this song because ______________.
  • The ______________ makes me feel ______________.
  • I listen to it when ______________.
  • Give two reasons. Talk about the sound AND the words.
  • Would you still like this song if the tempo were different? Why?
5

When did you first hear this song?

  • I first heard it in ______________ (year / place).
  • I heard it with ______________.
  • I first heard this song when ______________.
  • I was with ______________. We were ______________.
  • Tell the story of the first time you heard it. Where were you? Who were you with? What were you doing? How did you feel?
6

What is this song about? Why did the singer write it?

  • This song is about ______________.
  • The singer wrote it because ______________.
  • The main idea of this song is ______________.
  • The singer wrote it to ______________ (tell a story / say sorry / remember someone…).
  • What is the purpose of this song? Who is the singer talking to? What do you think happened in the singer's life before they wrote it?
7

Your favorite line

  • I like this line because it is ______________.
  • I like this line because ______________.
  • It makes me think of ______________.
  • Why is this line stronger than the other lines? What picture does it put in your head?

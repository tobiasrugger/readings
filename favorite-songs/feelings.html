Music Words 2: How does it make you feel?

Feeling words and reason words. After this lesson you can answer the big question: why do you like this song?

1

Meet the words

Read each word. Tap a card to see it in your language. Tap again to hide it.

2

Match the face

Tap a word on the left, then tap its picture on the right. The letter shows your answer.

3

Match the meaning

Tap a word, then tap the meaning that matches it.

4

Match your language

Match the English word to the word in your first language.

5

Put the words in groups

Tap a word, then tap the box where it belongs.

6

Finish the sentence

Use the word bank.

Use the word bank. Spelling counts.

Try it without looking at the word bank first.

    7

    Now you write

    Think of a song that gives you a strong feeling.

    • The song is called ______________.
    • This song makes me feel ______________.
    • I listen to this song when I am ______________.
    • I like this song because it is ______________.
    • When I hear ______________, I feel ______________.
    • It reminds me of ______________.
    • I listen to it when ______________.
    • The lyrics are about ______________.
    • Write 5 sentences. Explain how the song makes you feel and why.
    • Does the sound match the feeling? (For example: sad words with a happy beat.)
    • How did this song make you feel the FIRST time? Is it different now? Why?

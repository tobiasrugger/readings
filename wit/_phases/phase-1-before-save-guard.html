Sentence Work

Seven short activities about sentences. Do them in order. A sentence has a subject and a verb. It starts with a capital letter and ends with a period, a question mark, or an exclamation point.

The first try is your score. If you are wrong, you can try again.

1 · Sentence sort

Tap a sentence. Then tap the box where it belongs. Look at the end mark and the first word.

2 · Sentence scramble

Tap the words in the right order. Tap the end mark last. Capital letters are a clue.

3 · Sentence or fragment?

Is it a sentence (S) or a fragment (F)? A fragment is missing a subject or a verb. If it is a fragment, tap the repair.

4 · Sentence types

Look at the picture. Write one statement, one question, one command, and one exclamation.

5 · Because, but, so

Because gives a reason. But shows a contrast. So shows a result. First choose the word that fits. Then finish the three sentences.

6 · Make it pop

A kernel is a short, boring sentence. Pop it! 🍿 Answer the questions inside your new sentence. Keep the kernel words.

7 · Parallel revision

Rewrite the boring paragraph so it is better, but keep the same meaning. Combine sentences. Use names instead of he and she. Add details.

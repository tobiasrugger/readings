Spelling helper — teacher preview

What /readings/spell/spell.js does on student pages (T-273). Launch Plus lessons 1–5 use it now; other pages can load it.

1 · Voice check

Listen once and tell Claude which Mateo sounds right. Captions and the screen always show Mateo; only the voice changes.

Spelled names. The new way says letter names, each one starting on a fixed beat, so short letters like N and H don’t bunch up.

In Launch Plus Lesson 3, “Spell it” uses the new way. Story lines like “J - U - N” are read as “jay, you, en”.

2 · Writing tips (Launch Plus “Write”)

When a student writes a word that is probably the wrong one, a tip opens under the box with both words, pictures, meanings and translations. Nothing is marked wrong: the student decides. Each tip shown is counted for the dashboard.

A tip waits until the word is finished (a space or period after it).

3 · Spelling in parts

For long words: one row of boxes per part, 🔊 under each part, then the whole word once. Misses 1 and 2 on a part get a hint under it. On the 3rd miss, that part’s letters appear with 🔊 and “Look, listen, then type it yourself.” The same happens on the whole word. A word finished after it was shown still counts the full point; the dashboard just sees “shown”. Ready for the My Strengths words; a vocabulary page adds it to its “Listen and spell” (handover to that page’s chat).

4 · Any typed answer box

One box per letter. Try it: type a wrong word three times. Misses 1 and 2 get a hint; the 3rd miss shows the word big with 🔊. The student types it once to finish, for the full point.

5 · The tip pictures

All drawn for spell.js. Tell Claude any that should change.

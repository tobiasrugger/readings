Gradebook

Scores are 0–4 (— = not started, counts as 0). Tap a score to read that student's work. Tap an activity name to open it, or class to spread every sheet. Drag a column (or use ◀ ▶) to move it; untick the box to leave it out of the average. Your column order and ticks are remembered on this computer. R W S L = how much Reading, Writing, Speaking and Listening work each student has done in the last 30 days (a guess from the kind of activity, not a test) — the red word is what they probably need next. copy puts a column’s scores on the clipboard in this order, ready to paste into Synergy. 📊 beside an activity opens its whole-class dashboard; a tap on a score opens that dashboard at that student. ⚑ = stuck (three or more tries, still 0 or 1). Hover a score for its %, tries and last day worked. Blue columns are extra work you gave to chosen students: they only count for those students, and start unticked.

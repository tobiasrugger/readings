Part B · Columbus Day vs. Indigenous Peoples’ Day

In the beginning, the United States only had indigenous people called Native Americans. In 1492, a European man named Christopher Columbus came to North America. He went back to Europe and told people about it. As a result, thousands of European men and women came to North America. They had many diseases.

The European diseases killed millions of indigenous people. The Europeans also killed the indigenous people with weapons. When one group of people kills another group of people, it is called “genocide.”

Part C · Why some people are angry

In the United States, there is a holiday in October called Columbus Day, to celebrate Christopher Columbus. However, some people are angry that Columbus came to North America. People are angry because Columbus, and other Europeans who followed, killed millions of indigenous people. Therefore, some people do not want to celebrate Columbus Day. They call it Indigenous Peoples’ Day.

Part B · The army

Until 1995, indigenous people in Guatemala did not have the same rights as other people. In the 1970s and 1980s, many indigenous people protested for their rights. Rigoberta Menchú was one of these protesters. The Guatemalan army hurt and killed many protesters, including Rigoberta Menchú’s family members. The army tried to kill Rigoberta Menchú, too.

Part C · The book and the prize

But Rigoberta Menchú would not stop protesting, and she wrote a book about the problems in Guatemala. People all over the world read Rigoberta Menchú’s book and learned about the problems in Guatemala. Then they forced the Guatemalan government to give rights and equality to poor, indigenous people. She still works to help make the world a better place. She won the Nobel Peace Prize for her hard work.

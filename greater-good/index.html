The quiz

How kind are you to yourself?

Scientists made this quiz. It has 12 sentences about you. For each one, choose how often it is true for you. There are no right or wrong answers.

Take the quiz
  1. Press the yellow button. The quiz opens in a new tab.
  2. Need your language? Right-click on the quiz page (or tap with two fingers) and choose Translate.
  3. Answer questions 1 to 12. Questions 13 to 18 ask private things, like money and politics. You can skip them.
  4. Write down your score. Then come back to this tab.

You read the story. Now climb: words → sentences → paragraph → essay.

Eight rungs. Each rung is a little harder. Finish one rung to open the next.

2 · Sentences

Four short games about sentences from the story: what kind of sentence, sentence or fragment, because / but / so, and unscramble.

3 · Order

Tap a sentence, then tap the numbered box where it belongs. The second paragraph has one extra sentence that is not from the story. Leave it out.

4 · Paraphrase

Say it in your own words. Three steps for each sentence: swap two words, choose the new structure, then write your own version. The meter shows if your version is too close to the original.

5 · Paragraph

First choose the best topic sentence. Then write your own paragraph. The checklist fills in as you write.

6 · Revise

Good writers fix their writing. Fix these paragraphs first: capitals, run-on sentences, add a detail, cut extra sentences, verbs. Then revise your own paragraph.

7 · Essay

Build an outline first: hook, background, thesis, three claims with evidence, and a conclusion. Then build your draft and revise it.

8 · Intros & Conclusions

Short drills: pick the best hook, sort thesis statements from topic sentences, put an introduction in order, and rewrite a weak conclusion.

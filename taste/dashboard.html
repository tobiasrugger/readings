Misses are wrong taps before the right one; shown = the page pointed at the answer, still a full point (fair scores). Candy Lab and favorite are opinions, never wrong. A ✗ needs a note; the student sees a soft “Mr. Toby: try this again ✏️” box and gets the point back when they change the writing. 🎤 = the microphone heard at least half the sentence; ✋ = “I said it out loud” after two tries. My food is extra: it can fill a missing point, never lower a score.

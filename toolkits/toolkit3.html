TOOLKIT #3

Compare and contrast

To compare is to say how two things are similar. To contrast is to say how they are different. Almost every class asks you to do this.

Mr. Toby · ELD
WordIt meansMy language (ML)
compare say how things are the same
contrast say how things are different
similar almost the same
different not the same

1The Venn diagram

Two circles that overlap. Differences on the outside, similarities in the middle. Fill this in before you write, and the sentences come easily.

Try it: tap a word, then tap a circle. Tap a word inside a circle to take it back out. Add your own words too.

2Compare language

Sentences that say how things are the same. Start with the green ones.

easier 1) ______ and ______ are both ______.Romeo and Juliet are both teenagers. Apples and oranges are both fruits.
easier 2) ______ is like ______ because ______.A circle is like a basketball because they are both round. A drum is like a heart because they both keep a beat.
standard 3) An important similarity between ______ and ______ is that ______.An important similarity between English class and history class is that they both require a lot of writing. An important similarity between the Sun and a lightbulb is that they both give off light.
standard 4) Something that ______ and ______ have in common is ______.Something that Juliet and Tybalt have in common is that they are Capulets. Something that the Mission and Chinatown have in common is that they both have many immigrants.
challenge 5) ______ and ______ share the following traits: ______, ______, and ______.San Francisco and New York share the following traits: big, on the coast, and many immigrants. Basketball and soccer share the following traits: two teams, one ball, and a clock.
challenge 6) ______, just as ______.Romeo kills Tybalt, just as Tybalt kills Mercutio. A plant needs water, just as a person needs water.

is like means the same as is similar to. Both are correct.

3Contrast language

Sentences that say how things are different.

easier 1) ______ is/has ______, but ______ is/has ______.An apple is red, but a banana is yellow. A snake has no legs, but a mouse has four legs.
easier 2) ______ and ______ are different because ______ is/has ______ and ______ is/has ______.Apples and oranges are different because an apple is red and an orange is orange. Daniel's neighborhood and Norma's neighborhood are different because Daniel's neighborhood has cold weather and Norma's neighborhood has hot weather.
standard 3) An important difference between ______ and ______ is that ______.An important difference between a tree and a rock is that a tree is alive and a rock is not alive. An important difference between a guitar and a drum is that a guitar has strings and a drum does not.
standard 4) ______ and ______ differ because ______, while ______.Plays and stories differ because plays are performed, while stories are read. Sharks and dolphins differ because sharks are fish, while dolphins are mammals.
challenge 5) Unlike ______, who/which ______, ______.Unlike Galileo, which is a big school, my old school was very small. Unlike the Moon, which has no air, the Earth has an atmosphere.
challenge 6) The most notable contrast is that ______ is/has ______, whereas ______ is/has ______.The most notable contrast is that a circle is round, whereas a square has four sides. The most notable contrast is that the north had factories, whereas the south had farms.

4Signal words

These little words tell your reader what is coming next.

To show…Use these wordsExample
Similarboth, also, too, the same, as well, like, similar to, in the same wayBoth rocks are gray. The second rock is gray too.
Differentbut, however, while, whereas, unlike, on the other hand, different fromGold is soft, but iron is hard.
Careful with both. Both goes after the verb are: They are both tall. But before a normal verb: They both play soccer. Either order is fine at the start: Both boys are tall.

5Choose the right words

1. Water and ice ___ made of the same molecule.
2. A bat is a mammal, ___ a bird is not.
3. ___ a square, which has four sides, a triangle has three.
4. Soccer and basketball ___ because soccer uses feet, while basketball uses hands.
5. The violin is like the guitar ___ they both have strings.
6. Mercury and Venus ___ the following traits: rocky, small, and close to the Sun.

DESCRIBING PEOPLE · LESSON #1

Describing yourself

Answer questions about yourself using to be and to have. Choose a word, and the page builds the sentence with you.

Mr. Toby · ELD
Toolkits you need. Toolkit #2 — to be, to have, to do tells you when to say is and when to say has. Toolkit #1 — Pronouns tells you when to say he, she, it, or they. Open them in another tab and keep them open.
You are writing about yourself, so every sentence starts with I. I am…   I have…

1What do you have?

Hair and eyes are things on the body. You have them.

2What What are you like?

Height, age, and personality are what someone is like.

3More character traits

Choose the word that is more true about you. If neither one fits, choose the third option or write your own later.

4Say why, with "because"

A word by itself is a guess. A word plus evidence is a description. Add because and one detail.

%s
Pattern: ______ is/has ______ because ______.Evidence means a real detail: something they do, own, say, or made.
Write
Write six sentences with because. Use the words you chose above.

5Harder words

These words describe people more exactly. Use them if you are ready. Tap a word to see what it means.

Choose from these, or use your own: vengefulforgivingstubbornflexiblebravecowardlyrespectfulrudepatientcurioushonestorganizedcreativecalmcompetitiveloyal
Challenge
Write five more sentences using harder words. Every sentence needs because and a real detail.

6Read it out loud

Read your sentences to your partner. Then answer this.

Reflect
Which of your sentences is the best one? Copy it here and say why it is the best.
A good sentence has the right verb, a real detail, and a capital letter at the start.

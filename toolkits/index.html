Unit: describing yourself and others

Do these in order. Each one uses the toolkits above.

  1. Lesson #1 — Describing yourself
    Choose words about you. The page builds the sentence with you. Uses Toolkit #2.
  2. Lesson #2 — Describing a classmate
    Ask the questions, write the answers, then ask why. Uses Toolkits #1 and #2.
  3. Lesson #3 — Describing a character
    A video game character, a person from a book, or an athlete. Then draw and label.
  4. Lesson #3b — Venn diagrams and sentences
    You and your classmate. You and your character. Sixteen sentences. Uses Toolkit #3.
  5. Lesson #4 — Compare and contrast practice 1
    Yao and Chris. The man and the girl. Mario and Superman.
  6. Lesson #5 — Compare and contrast practice 2
    A dog and a cat. Your old school and Galileo. Your friends there and here.

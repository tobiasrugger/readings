Compare and contrast

1Two things under the microscope

Science asks you to compare constantly: two states of matter, two planets, two animals. Fill the Venn first, then write.

Part A · Solid and liquid

Tap a word, then tap the circle where it belongs.

Compare
Write two sentences that say how a solid and a liquid are similar.
Frames: ______ and ______ are both ______.    An important similarity between ______ and ______ is that ______.
Contrast
Write two sentences that say how they are different.
Frames: ______ is ______, but ______ is ______.    ______ and ______ differ because ______, while ______.

Part B · The Sun and the Moon

Both
Write three sentences about the Sun and the Moon. At least one must compare and at least one must contrast.
Try the challenge frame: Unlike ______, which ______, ______.

Part C · Your own pair

Choose two things from any science class: two animals, two planets, two kinds of rock, two body systems.
Science
Write your two things, then three sentences comparing and contrasting them.
Line 1: name the two things. Lines 2 and 3: your sentences.

2Two places, two ways of life

History is full of pairs: north and south, city and countryside, then and now. Compare them and the reason for a conflict often becomes clear.

Part A · Farms and factories

Compare
Write two sentences that say how a farm and a factory are similar.
Frame: Something that ______ and ______ have in common is ______.
Contrast
Write two sentences that say how they are different.
Frame: The most notable contrast is that ______ is/has ______, whereas ______ is/has ______.

Part B · Your country and the United States

Add your own words. This is your life, so your words matter more than the ones in the bank.
History
Write four sentences comparing and contrasting the two countries. Two compare, two contrast.
Example: My country and the United States are both large. An important difference is that my country is warm all year.

Part C · Two leaders

Challenge
Choose two leaders from any time or place — presidents, kings, activists, generals. Write three sentences.
Try: ______, just as ______.    Unlike ______, who ______, ______.

3Two shapes, two numbers

In math, "compare" has an exact job: say what two things share and what separates them. Be precise.

Part A · Square and circle

Compare
Write two sentences that say how a square and a circle are similar.
Frame: ______ and ______ are both ______.
Contrast
Write two sentences that say how they are different.
Frame: ______ has ______, but ______ has ______.

Part B · Even and odd numbers

Math
Write three sentences about even and odd numbers. At least one compares and one contrasts.
Frame: An important difference between ______ and ______ is that ______.

Part C · Two ways to solve

Sometimes two students get the same answer using different methods. That is a compare-and-contrast situation.
Math
Think of a problem you and a classmate solved differently. Write three sentences comparing your two methods.
Example: We both got 20. I multiplied first, but she added first. Our methods differ because I used the order of operations, while she worked left to right.

4Two paintings, two sports

Comparing is how you explain taste. Instead of "I like it," you say exactly what is different about it.

Part A · Guitar and drum

Compare
Write two sentences that say how a guitar and a drum are similar.
Frame: ______ and ______ share the following traits: ______, ______, and ______.
Contrast
Write two sentences that say how they are different.

Part B · Soccer and basketball

PE
Write four sentences about soccer and basketball. Two compare, two contrast.
Frame: ______ and ______ differ because ______, while ______.

Part C · Two songs

Choose one song in English and one song in your language. You are the expert on both.
Music
Write your two songs, then three sentences comparing and contrasting them.
Think about: fast or slow, happy or sad, instruments, what the words are about.

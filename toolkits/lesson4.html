Compare and contrast
practice

Mr. Toby · ELD
compare = say how things are the same.    contrast = say how things are different.    A Venn diagram helps you organize both before you write. Open Toolkit #3 in another tab.

1Yao and Chris

Look at the picture your teacher is showing. Yao is a very tall basketball player. Chris is much shorter, and he also plays sports. Put these three words in the diagram: tall, short, athletic.

Now finish these two sentences.

1. and are both .
2. is , but is .

2The man and the girl

Put these four words in the diagram: young, old, musical, serious. Then add more of your own.

Frames from Toolkit #3.
Same: ___ and ___ are both ___.  |  ___ is like ___ because ___.  |  An important similarity between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  Something that ___ and ___ have in common is ___.
Different: ___ is ___, but ___ is ___.  |  An important difference between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  ___ and ___ differ because ___, while ___.  |  Unlike ___, who/which ___, ___.
Write
Write 3 sentences about the man and the girl. Use compare and contrast language.

3Mario and Superman

You already know these two characters. Put at least six words in the diagram. Think about: their jobs, their clothes, their powers, where they live, what they look like.

Write
Write 3 sentences about Mario and Superman. At least one must compare and one must contrast.
Example: Mario and Superman are both heroes. An important difference between them is that Superman can fly and Mario cannot.

4Check your own work

Reflect
Look back at your nine sentences. How many compare and how many contrast? Count them.
If you have zero of one kind, go back and change a sentence.

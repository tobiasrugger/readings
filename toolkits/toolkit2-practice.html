1Describe what you observe

A scientist writes what something IS and what it HAS. Those two verbs carry almost every observation.

Two columns, every time you observe something.
It is … clear, blue, warm, solid, heavy, alive
It has … bubbles, layers, a shell, six legs, a rough surface

Part A · is, are, has, or have?

1. The magnet ___ strong.
2. The two beakers ___ the same size.
3. A plant cell ___ a thick wall.
4. Insects ___ six legs.
5. Ice ___ not a liquid.
6. The rock ___ three visible layers.
7. Oxygen and nitrogen ___ gases.
8. This leaf ___ small holes in it.

Part B · Start the question

1. ___ the liquid clear?
2. ___ the rock have layers?
3. ___ these two samples the same?
4. ___ magnets attract plastic?
5. ___ the water boiling yet?
6. ___ your plant have new leaves?

Part C · Observe something real

Observe
Pick any object near you right now. Write two sentences with is and two sentences with has.
Example: This pencil is yellow. It is about 15 centimeters long. It has a pink eraser. It has teeth marks on the side.
Ask
Now write two questions a scientist could ask about that object. One must start with Is. One must start with Does.
Example: Is it heavy? Does it float?

2Who were they? What did the place have?

Describe a place, a person, or a civilization. Use IS for what it is like and HAS for what it contains.

Try this pattern on any civilization.
Egypt is in Africa. It has a long river. The pyramids are very old. They have stone walls.

Part A · is, are, has, or have?

1. The Great Pyramid ___ more than four thousand years old.
2. Egypt ___ a very long river.
3. The Romans ___ famous for their roads.
4. Many old cities ___ high walls around them.
5. A king ___ power, but he ___ not always kind.
6. My country ___ a flag with three colors.
7. These two maps ___ different.
8. The museum ___ a room full of Egyptian art.

Part B · Start the question

1. ___ these two countries neighbors?
2. ___ the museum have Egyptian art?
3. ___ the flag have a star on it?
4. ___ the capital city near the ocean?
5. ___ people still speak that language?

Part C · Describe a place you know

History
Describe your home country, your city, or the place your family is from. Write two sentences with is and two with has.
Example: My country is in Central America. It is warm all year. It has two oceans. It has many volcanoes.
History
Now describe one important person from that place. Two sentences with is or was, two with has or had.

3A shape IS something and HAS something

Every shape has a name and a list of parts. That is exactly what to be and to have are for.

The math pattern.
A square is a four-sided shape. It has four equal sides. It has four right angles.

Part A · is, are, has, or have?

1. A triangle ___ three sides.
2. A circle ___ round.
3. Six and eight ___ even numbers.
4. A rectangle ___ four right angles.
5. Zero ___ not a positive number.
6. These two angles ___ equal.
7. A cube ___ six faces.
8. Prime numbers ___ only two factors.

Part B · Start the question

1. ___ this shape have four equal sides?
2. ___ the two triangles congruent?
3. ___ seven a prime number?
4. ___ all rectangles have four sides?
5. ___ your answer the same as mine?

Part C · Define a shape

Math
Choose a shape: triangle, square, circle, rectangle, hexagon, cube, or cylinder. Write four sentences that define it. Use is twice and has twice.
Example: A cylinder is a solid shape. It is like a can. It has two circles. It has one curved side.
Math
Write two questions that would help someone guess your shape. One starts with Is. One starts with Does.
Example: Is it flat? Does it have straight sides?

4Describe what you see, hear, and play

A painting is something and has something. So does a song, a team, and a pair of shoes.

Words for art and music:brightdarkloudquietfastslowa strong beatwarm colorscool colorsa rough surfacea steady rhythm

Part A · is, are, has, or have?

1. The painting ___ a lot of dark blue.
2. The song ___ very fast.
3. The drums ___ the loudest instrument in the band.
4. My running shoes ___ good grip.
5. A guitar ___ six strings.
6. Our team ___ ready for the game.
7. These two songs ___ the same rhythm.
8. The sculpture ___ made of clay.

Part B · Ask your partner

1. ___ you have a partner?
2. ___ she on our team?
3. ___ this song have words?
4. ___ we ready to start?
5. ___ you play an instrument?
6. ___ the ball flat?

Part C · Describe a song

Music
Choose a song you love, in any language. Write four sentences. Use is twice and has twice.
Example: The song is slow and sad. It is from my country. It has a guitar and a drum. It has only four lines.

Part D · Describe a sport

PE
Choose a sport. Write four sentences using is, are, has, and have — one of each.
Example: Soccer is a team sport. The rules are simple. Each team has eleven players. The players have special shoes.

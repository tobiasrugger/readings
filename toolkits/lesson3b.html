1You and your classmate

Put 12 pieces of information into the diagram: at least 2 ways you are the same, and at least 2 ways you are different.

Tap a word, then tap a circle. Tap "Add your own word" for anything not in the list — your country, your sport, your favorite food.

2Eight sentences about you and your classmate

Frames from Toolkit #3.
Same: ___ and ___ are both ___.  |  ___ is like ___ because ___.  |  An important similarity between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  Something that ___ and ___ have in common is ___.
Different: ___ is ___, but ___ is ___.  |  An important difference between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  ___ and ___ differ because ___, while ___.  |  Unlike ___, who/which ___, ___.
Write
Write 8 sentences. At least 3 must compare and at least 3 must contrast.
Example: Ana and I are both sixteen. An important difference between us is that she has curly hair and I have straight hair.

3You and your character

Now compare yourself to the character from Lesson #3. Again, 12 pieces of information.

4Eight sentences about you and your character

Frames from Toolkit #3.
Same: ___ and ___ are both ___.  |  ___ is like ___ because ___.  |  An important similarity between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  Something that ___ and ___ have in common is ___.
Different: ___ is ___, but ___ is ___.  |  An important difference between ___ and ___ is that ___.  |  ___ and ___ differ because ___, while ___.  |  Unlike ___, who/which ___, ___.
Write
Write 8 sentences. At least 3 must compare and at least 3 must contrast.
Example: Link and I are both quiet. Unlike Link, who is brave, I am sometimes afraid.

5One paragraph

Challenge
Put four of your best sentences together into one paragraph. Start with: My classmate and I are similar in some ways and different in others.

What do the seven role badges mean?

Everyone at the table gets one. Your job is not to know the answer — your job is to make sure your whole table has one.

CChecker for Understanding

Make sure everyone understands. Nobody copies. Ask: “Do you understand the question?”

IInference Maker

Make sure everyone makes a guess based on clues. Different answers are fine.

NConnector

Make sure everyone connects it to their own life, another book, or the world. These are personal — they should be different.

PPredictor

Make sure everyone guesses what happens next, before you read on.

VVisualizer

Make sure everyone describes the picture in their head. Point at panels.

LLanguage Looker

Make sure everyone knows the words. You look them up first.

QCritical Questioner

Make sure everyone thinks hard about the big question. There is no single right answer.

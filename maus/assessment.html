Part 3 — The big question worth the most

Was Vladek a survivor because he was lucky, or because he was smart?

There is no right answer. There is only a good argument. Use your book.

Level 1 — build it piece by piece

1. Choose one.
2. One thing that happened in the book that proves it.
On page ______, Vladek ______.
3. Another thing.
Also, on page ______, ______.
4. Now put your four sentences together into a paragraph.
I think Vladek was ______. On page ______, ______. Also ______. This shows Vladek was ______.

Level 2 — one paragraph

  • A topic sentence that says what you think.
  • Two pieces of evidence from Maus, with page numbers.
  • After each piece, one sentence saying why it proves your point.
  • A last sentence that closes it.
Vladek survived mainly because he was ______. For example, on page ______, he ______. This shows ______. Another example is ______. This proves ______. Therefore, ______.

Level 3 — argue it properly

  • A claim that takes a real position — not “both a little.”
  • Three pieces of evidence from three different chapters, at least one from Maus II.
  • A counter-argument: what would someone on the other side say? Answer them.
  • Consider: Vladek himself says he was lucky. Should we believe him?

Check your own paragraph first

Part 4 — Figurative language

A simile about any character in Maus. Use like or as.

A metaphor about any character. Say one thing is another.

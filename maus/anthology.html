Whose stories get written down?

Art Spiegelman spent thirteen years asking his father questions. That is the only reason Vladek Spiegelman’s story exists on paper.

This term, every student in this class found one person and asked them the questions nobody had asked. Some of these interviews happened in Spanish, Cantonese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Mam. Every student in this book is also the translator of their own story.

Part D — Write (20 points)

Prompt: The United States government took away many things from the Wakatsuki family — their home, their boat, their neighbors, even their name. Choose the loss you think hurt the family most. Explain what was taken, how the family reacted, and why that loss mattered more than the others.

You must use: at least two details from the book, and one detail from the film.

Planner — do this first

1. My answer (thesis): The worst loss for the Wakatsuki family was ____________ because ____________.
2. Reason 1 + evidence from the book:

3. Reason 2 + evidence from the book:

4. Evidence from the film:

5. Why the other losses hurt less:

Paragraph 2: First, ____________. In Chapter ___, ____________. This shows ____________.

Paragraph 4: Some people might say ____________, but ____________. That is why ____________.

Scoring guide for Part D

4 — ExceedsClear answer to the question. Three or more specific details from the book and film. Explains why, not just what. Past tense is correct almost everywhere.
3 — MeetsClear answer. Two book details and one film detail. Some explanation. Past tense mostly correct.
2 — ApproachingAn answer is there but the evidence is general ("it was bad"). Fewer than three details. Verb tense shifts.
1 — BeginningRetells the story without answering the question, or uses no evidence.

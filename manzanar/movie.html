The film: Farewell to Manzanar (1976), directed by John Korty. Jeanne Wakatsuki Houston helped write it. It was made for television and runs about two hours. Yuki Shimoda plays Papa; Nobu McCarthy plays Mama; Pat Morita and Mako are also in it.

Your job: The movie is not the same as the book. Some scenes are moved. Some are invented. Some things in the book are left out. Watch for the differences — that is your essay evidence.

One warning about times: the times below come from Mr. Toby's copy of the film. If your copy starts differently, the scene is still in the same order.

Words for this film — tap any of them: internment · loyalty oath · inu · riot · protest · block · mess hall · enlist · barracks · ration

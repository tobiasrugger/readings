1The story

Kim is nine years old. Her family is from Vietnam. Now they live in Cleveland.

Kim's father died in Vietnam. He died before Kim was born. Kim never met him.

Her father was a farmer. He grew food.

Near her home there is an empty lot. It is full of junk.

Early one morning, Kim goes to the lot. She has six beans. She digs small holes. She plants the beans.

She does not tell anyone. She wants her father to see her. She wants him to know she is his daughter.

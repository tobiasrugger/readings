Stromae — What does the video say about gender?

Not signed in

Before we watch

Stromae is a Belgian singer. His father was from Rwanda; his mother is Belgian. He makes videos that look fun but say something serious. Today we watch two of them and ask one question: what is this video saying about men and women?

Agree or disagree

There is no right answer. Choose what you really think. We will come back to these at the end.

Two words we will use all day

Gender is not only about bodies. It is also about unwritten rules: who should be strong, who should cry, who should stay home, who should dance.

A stereotype is a simple idea about a whole group. All men are… All women are… Both videos play with stereotypes on purpose.

Boys are supposed to ______. Girls are supposed to ______.

Stromae's own father was killed in the Rwandan genocide when Stromae was a child. He barely knew him. Watch the boy and the man in the chair.

Tous les mêmes (“All the same”)

Stromae plays two people at once. Half of his face and body is a man; half is a woman. They are arguing — and they are the same person. Watch the lighting, the makeup, the shoulders.

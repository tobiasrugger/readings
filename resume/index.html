My Résumé — Mr. Toby

1. Heading

Every résumé starts with how to reach you. Use an email you actually check. heading

2. Objective optional

One sentence: what job or program do you want right now? Change it every time you apply for something new. objective

3. Education

Galileo first. Then your school in your home country if you want. Only put GPA if it is 3.0 or higher. education

4. Work Experience

Paid jobs, internships, and family business counts. Work you did in another country counts too. work experience

5. Activities & Volunteering

Sports, clubs, church, translating for your family, taking care of siblings, tutoring a friend. This all counts. volunteer

6. Awards & Honors

Honor roll, perfect attendance, certificates, student of the month, sports medals. award

7. Skills & Languages

Your home language is a professional skill. Always list it. skill

⭐ Brag Bank

Did something good this week? Write it here fast. Don't worry about grammar. You will forget it by senior year if you don't write it down. Later you can turn a note into a real résumé line.

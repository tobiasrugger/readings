Partner interviews · Mr. Toby

How it works
  1. Open an interview on your Chromebook. Choose your period and your name.
  2. Walk to a classmate at another table. Choose their name.
  3. Listen first. Then tap the red button and say your sentence. Your classmate records their sentences too.
  4. Tap Switch so your classmate asks you. Then find a new classmate.

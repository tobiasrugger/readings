Words to know

town
a small city; a place where people live
market
a place where people buy and sell things
shopkeeper
the person who works in a shop and sells things
money
coins and paper you use to buy things
bag
something you carry things in
smiles
makes a happy face
gives
puts something into another person's hand
happy
feeling good; glad
walks
goes somewhere using her feet
Every Monday
on Monday of every week; again and again
puts
places something somewhere
syllable
one beat in a word (pen-guin has two)

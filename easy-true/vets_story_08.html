Story 08

Stuck!

Three men move a house. Then they have a problem.

Page 1 of 3
Panel 1 — A truck pulls a house
Three men are in a truck. The men are Sam, Joe, and Tom. The truck is pulling a house.
QuestionQ1
What is the truck doing?
Page 2 of 3
Panel 2 — STUCK!
The men drive under a bridge. The truck stops. Why? The house is too tall! The house is stuck under the bridge!
QuestionQ2
What is stuck?
PredictNo right answer
The house is stuck under the bridge. How can the men get the house out?

Page 3 of 3
Panel 3 — Tom looks at the tires
Tom looks at the truck's tires. He has an idea! He lets air out of the tires. The other men help. The men let air out of all the tires.
Panel 4 — They drive away!
The tires get smaller. Now the truck and house are not so tall. There is half an inch between the house and the bridge. The house is not stuck! The men drive away with the house.

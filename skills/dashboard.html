🧭 Skill Map

What each student has practised and shown in listening, speaking, reading, writing and how English works. Every activity on the site is tagged to skills, so this fills in by itself. Your Read-aloud Checks count straight away.

1 Which class?

How “practised”, “shown” and “needs work” are decided

Practised: the student did an activity that works on this skill (each activity on each day counts once). Shown: a score of 3 or more (75%+) on two different days, or one Read-aloud Check from you scored 3+. Needs work: practised 3 or more times, not shown, and the last three scores average under 60%. Activities with no score count as practice but never as “shown”. Each period uses its course’s skills (101: periods 1, 4, 6 · 103: periods 3, 5); a student working at the other level can be switched with the 101/103 button in their report.

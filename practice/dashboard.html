Practice log — time, 🔊 and translations

What this shows

Every student page counts three things, per student, per page, per day:

⏱ Active minutes — the page was open on screen and the student touched, typed or scrolled in the last minute. A page left open in another tab counts nothing.

🔊 Listens — the student tapped something and sound played (a word, a sentence, a recording). One per tap.

🌐 Translations — the student tapped something and asked for a translation. Directions translated on arrival don’t count.

Counting started Nov 2026 (T-148). Nothing is counted on the sign-in screen, in teacher view, or for test runs. Tap a student to see their pages, day by day.

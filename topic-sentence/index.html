Topic Sentences

What is a topic sentence? Learn it, choose it, write it. About 20 minutes.

A paragraph is a group of sentences about one idea.

The topic sentence is the first sentence. It says the big idea of the whole paragraph.

The other sentences give examples or reasons. They are the details.

🟨 topic sentence = big idea  ·  ⬜ details = first, second, finally…

Four parts: Learn, Choose, Write, Done. Tap an answer. If you are wrong, try again, but the first try is your score.

1 · Learn

Read two paragraphs. The first sentence is the topic sentence. Your language is below. In the second paragraph, you choose the topic sentence.

2 · Choose

Read the details. Which sentence is the topic sentence? It must be about all the details, not one.

3 · Write

Now you write the topic sentence. Use a frame if you want. Capital letter, period.

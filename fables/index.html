    Story elements

    Every story has these five parts. Use the sentence starters if you need them.

    1. Characters the people or animals

    The characters in this story are ___ and ___.

    2. Setting where and when

    The story takes place in ___. It happens ___.

    3. Conflict the problem

    The problem is that ___.

    4. Plot what happens, in order

    First ___. Then ___. Next ___. After that ___. Finally ___.

    5. Resolution how it ends

    At the end, ___.

    The moral what the story teaches us

    This story teaches us that ___.

How to use should

should + the plain verb. No to, and no -s on the verb.

You should go to Flower Piano.  ✓
You should to go to Flower Piano.  ✗
He should goes go early.  ✗

To say no: You should not clean on Saturday. In speaking we say shouldn’t.

Softer ways to give advice: Maybe you should… · I think you should… · You could…

Words for saying what you think

You liked it: That sounds fun. · I would love to… · I have never… · I want to try…

You did not: That sounds boring to me. · I would rather… · I do not really like… · It is not my thing.

Saying something sounds boring is polite in English as long as you give a reason. Cleaning sounds boring to me because I do it every weekend already.

If you are stuck on the last one

All of these are correct ways to talk about the future. Use whichever sounds best to you:

I will see my friend. · I am going to see my friend. · I’m going to see my friend.
I might see my friend. (not sure) · I plan to see my friend. · If I feel motivated, I will…

And these to say when: Tonight… · Saturday… · Saturday night… · Sunday…

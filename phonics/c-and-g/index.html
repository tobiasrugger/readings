The trick: look at the letter after C or G. It tells you the sound.

C

c + e, i, y → /s/ ☀️ sun
c + a, o, u, or a consonant → /k/ 🪁 kite

G

g + e, i, y → /j/ 👖 jeans
g + a, o, u, or a consonant → /g/ 🐐 goat

🎨 Shades of Meaning playground

Teacher playground, no sign-in. Dotted-underlined words have shades. 📖 Dictionary = tap a word for a simple meaning + translation (the page's normal tooltip). 🎨 Shades = tap a word for the continuum. Open with ?mode=shades to start in Shades. Underlines only show in Shades mode. In either mode, typing a basic word in the Write box shows a ✨ Bigger word? chip.

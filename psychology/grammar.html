Grammar Gym

Six workouts. Each one gives you sentences you actually need for this unit. Do one workout per week, or do them all. Check yourself as you go.

Workout 1 · BE + Personality Adjectives

To describe a personality in English, you need BE and an adjective.

SubjectBEAdjective
Iamorganized.
You / We / Theyarecalm.
He / She / Chato / My motherisshy.

Careful. In English you cannot leave BE out.
✗ He organized.  ✓ He is organized.
✗ I very shy.  ✓ I am very shy.

Write am, is, or are.

1. Chato ______ not ashamed of being Mexican.
2. I ______ usually calm and relaxed.
3. The boy and his mother ______ very different.
4. My ideal self ______ braver than my actual self.
5. We ______ studying psychology this year.
6. My friends ______ more open to new experiences than I ______ .
✍ Write your own.
Write 3 sentences: I am ______ . My friend is ______ . My family is ______ .
Write 4 sentences about 4 different people using BE + adjective.
Write 4 sentences, each with a different subject, and add a because clause to two of them.

Workout 2 · Simple Past — Telling a Story

Both our stories happened in the past. To retell them, use the simple past.

Regular verbs: add -ed.   walk → walked  ·  want → wanted  ·  zip → zipped  ·  cry → cried

Irregular verbs change completely. These are the ones you need:

NowPastNowPastNowPast
wearworegetgotgowent
taketookputputsaysaid
telltoldrunraneatate
feelfeltknowknewgrowgrew
writewrotequitquitseesaw

Write the past form.

1. The boy (wear) ______ the jacket for three years.
2. Chato (quit) ______ school in the morning.
3. His mother (say) ______ that children in Mexico would love it.
4. He (want) ______ a black leather jacket.
5. Chato (write) ______ his name all over the city.
6. The dog (bite) ______ him and (tear) ______ the sleeve.
7. He (feel) ______ ashamed at lunch.
8. Rabbit (run) ______ down the alley.
✍ Retell it.
Write 3 past-tense sentences about “The Jacket.”
Retell Part 3 of “The Jacket” in 5 past-tense sentences.
Retell one full part of either story in past tense, using at least 5 irregular verbs. Underline them.

Workout 3 · Comparatives — Comparing Your Selves

To compare two things, use a comparative.

Adjective lengthRuleExample
1 syllableadd -er + thanMy ideal self is braver than my actual self.
2 syllables ending in -y-y → -ier + thanChato is angrier than the boy.
2 or more syllablesmore + adjective + thanMy ought self is more patient than I am.
irregulargood → better, bad → worseHis school year got worse than before.

Also useful: “My actual self is very different from my ideal self.”  ·  “They are similar to each other.”  ·  “In comparison, my ought self is…”

Write the comparative form.

1. (tall) My brother is ______ than me.
2. (organized) My ideal self is ______ than my actual self.
3. (happy) Chato seems ______ at the end than at the start.
4. (good) His new jacket was not ______ than his old one.
5. (confident) Chato is ______ than the boy in “The Jacket.”
6. (young) The boy was ______ than Chato.
✍ Compare your selves.
My ideal self is ______ than my actual self.
Write 3 comparative sentences about your actual, ideal, and ought selves.
Write 4 comparative sentences: two about your own selves, two comparing Chato and the boy in “The Jacket.”

Workout 4 · Want To vs. Should — Ideal and Ought

This is the grammar of the three selves. Get this right and you can say almost anything in this unit.

SelfGrammarExample
ActualBE / simple presentI am shy. I speak two languages.
Idealwant to / would like to / wish I were + base formI want to be braver. I would like to travel.
Oughtshould / have to / am supposed to + base formI should study more. My family thinks I have to help at home.

Careful. After a modal (should, must, can), use the base form with no to and no -s.
✗ I should to study.  ✗ He should studies.  ✓ He should study.

Choose the right form.

1. I want ______ more patient.
2. My parents think I should ______ a doctor.
3. Chato’s father wanted him ______ a lawyer.
4. The boy ______ a black leather jacket, but he got a green one.
5. A good son ______ say thank you to his mother.
6. Which sentence describes an ideal self?
✍ Three selves, three sentences.
I am ______ . I want to ______ . I should ______ .
Write six sentences: three about yourself, three about the boy in “The Jacket.” Vary the structures.

Workout 5 · Because and So — the Analysis Move

Analysis (the A in PEA) explains why. Two connectors do most of the work.

WordWhat comes after itExample
becausethe cause (the reason)He hated the jacket because it was not what he asked for.
sothe effect (the result)The jacket was ugly, so he took it off at lunch.
because ofa noun, not a sentenceBecause of the jacket, he felt invisible.

Careful. because + sentence. because of + noun.
✗ Because of he was poor.  ✓ Because he was poor.  ✓ Because of his family’s money problems.

Write because, so, or because of.

1. Chato was alone, ______ he wrote his name everywhere.
2. He felt like a nobody ______ his gang was gone.
3. ______ the green jacket, the boy felt ashamed for three years.
4. His ought self is loud, ______ he says thank you even when he is angry.
5. The gap is painful ______ his ideal self is very far from his actual self.
6. ______ peer pressure, many teenagers change how they dress.
✍ Write analysis sentences.
This evidence is important because ______ .  /  He felt ______ , so he ______ .
Write three analysis sentences about “The Jacket.” Each must name a psychological concept (self, gap, self-esteem, influence) and use because or so.

Workout 6 · Talking About Influence

Four patterns. Learn all four and you never have to repeat yourself.

PatternExample
X influences me to + base formMy sister influences me to work harder.
X makes me + base form (no to)Music makes me feel calm.
Because of X, I + verbBecause of my father, I speak two languages.
X has a big / small influence on meMy friends have a big influence on my clothes.

Careful. make takes no to. ✗ It makes me to feel sad. ✓ It makes me feel sad.

Fix or complete each sentence.

1. My mother influences me ______ study every night.
2. The gossip at school makes him ______ ashamed.
3. ______ his gang, Chato felt safe. (2 words)
4. Which sentence is correct?
5. Which sentence is correct?
6. Which sentence is correct?
✍ Write about your influences.
Use two patterns. ______ influences me to ______ . ______ makes me ______ .
Write 3 sentences. Use 3 different patterns.
Write 4 sentences using all four patterns, about four different influences. Then rank them by magnitude.

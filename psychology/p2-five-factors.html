Easier

Your personality is the way you usually think, feel, and act.

Psychologists say every personality has five factors. A factor is one important part. Every person is a combination of these five.

You are not “good” or “bad” at a factor. There is no wrong answer. Be honest.

Standard & Challenge

Psychologists believe there are five important factors in everyone’s personality. They believe every person is a combination of these factors: all the choices you make show what kind of person you are.

Read each question and choose the answer that is right for you. There are no wrong answers, so please be honest about your own personality.

One caution. A score is not a cage. Psychologists describe the five factors as tendencies — what you usually do — not rules about what you must do. A 2 on self-discipline today does not predict your whole life. And the same person can be a 4 in one setting and a 1 in another. Notice, as you answer, whether you are thinking of school-you, home-you, or friends-you.

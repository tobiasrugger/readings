Who am I?

How do I influence other people?

How do other people influence me?

In this unit you will:

  • Learn what psychology is and why it matters to your life.
  • Describe your own personality using the Big Five model.
  • Read two short stories: The Somebody and The Jacket.
  • Learn the three selves: actual, ideal, and ought.
  • Map the influences on your life — family, friends, culture.
  • Write PEA paragraphs with a point, evidence, and analysis.

Part E · Short Answer 12 points

Answer in one or two sentences. Use the frames.

1. What is one thing Chato and the boy in “The Jacket” have in common?
Both boys ______ .
2. Name one influence on your life and say what it makes you do.
______ influences me to ______ .
3. Describe your actual self in one sentence.
My actual self is ______ .
4. Why is it useful to know about your three selves?
It is useful because ______ .

Answer each in two to four sentences. Include evidence.

1. Both Chato and the boy in “The Jacket” care a lot about how other people see them. Give one example from each story.
2. Choose one Big Five factor and use it to describe Chato. Give evidence from the story.
3. Explain the difference between an ideal self and an ought self. Give one example of each from your own life.
4. Name the influence with the biggest magnitude in your life and explain how it changes your choices.

Answer each in a developed response with textual evidence. Short quotes where useful.

1. Chato makes himself visible; the boy in “The Jacket” tries to disappear. Argue which boy is more controlled by other people’s opinions, using evidence from both stories.
2. The boy blames the jacket and blames his mother. Using the three selves, explain what he is actually angry at.
3. Mr. Calderon tells Chato that maybe he puts the Sierra mark on his own house. If that is true, what does it reveal about the relationship between Chato’s actual self and his ideal self?
4. A person raised in a collaborative culture and a person raised in an independent culture read “The Jacket.” How might their reactions to the mother differ? Explain using unit vocabulary.

Part F · Paragraph 30 points

Write a PEA paragraph (4–5 sentences): Describe your actual self, and explain why it is important to know it. Use the frames below.

(P) There are many ways I can describe myself. (E) One important thing about my actual self is ______ . An example from my life is ______ . (A) It is important to know my actual self because ______ .

Write a PEEA paragraph (6–8 sentences): Describe the three selves of the boy in “The Jacket” and explain why they matter in his life. Use at least two pieces of evidence from the text.

Write a PEEEA paragraph (9–12 sentences): Choose either Chato or the boy in “The Jacket.” Argue which force shapes him more — his own ideal self, or the influence of the people around him. Use three pieces of evidence, at least two with short quotes.

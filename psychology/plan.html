Teacher Plan — 8 Weeks

Summative task: a psychological profile of a story protagonist — Chato or the boy in “The Jacket” — built from Big Five traits, the three selves, and influence mapping, and delivered as a PEA/PEEEA paragraph plus a group poster or slide set.

Differentiation Moves Built Into the Pages

  • Three level buttons change the text, the question stems, and the amount of scaffolding — not the content. Everyone works on the same ideas.
  • Summary boxes accept L1. Newcomers may summarize in their home language; the English box stays open for later.
  • Tap-to-translate on every glossed word, into 12 languages, cached so it is fast the second time.
  • Sentence frames appear at Easier and Standard, disappear at Challenge.
  • Auto-scored checks give instant feedback on vocabulary and grammar so you can spend conference time on the writing.
  • Print CSS on every page: all three levels print at once so a packet works for a sub day or a no-device day.

Group Roles for the Question Protocol

RoleJobLanguage they need
ReaderReads the part out loud, twice.Fluency, no writing pressure. Good newcomer entry.
FinderPoints to the line in the text that answers the question.“It says here…” “Look at line ___.”
ExplainerSays why that line is the answer.“This shows… because…”
RecorderWrites the group answer. Everyone copies it.Full sentences, capitals and periods.
ReporterShares with the class.“Our group thinks… Our evidence is…”

What Goes in the Binder

Every page prints with a name line at the top and a binder number line at the bottom, matching the paper packet system. Print order: p1, p2, p3, p4, p5, p6, grammar, writing, assessment.
